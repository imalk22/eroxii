The story

It started in 2021 with Facebook and Instagram campaigns for local businesses. Clients kept asking for more: a better page, better posts, a website that actually converted the ad traffic, and a system to handle the orders that followed.

So the studio grew to cover the whole journey, from the first ad someone sees to the website, the POS at the counter, and the report that shows what worked. Along the way came NFT collections for international clients, POS rollouts for retail and restaurants, and free public tools like TinWise.
