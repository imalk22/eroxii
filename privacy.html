Privacy, in plain English.

Last updated: November 2026

What this site collects

Nothing is stored on this website's servers. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies and no analytics scripts.

When you contact us

  • Contact form: the form opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.
  • WhatsApp: buttons open WhatsApp with an optional pre-written message. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that chat.
  • Assistant: the on-site assistant runs entirely in your browser. It answers from this site's content and does not send what you type anywhere unless you choose to continue on WhatsApp.

Stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser (localStorage) so the site remembers it. You can clear it at any time by clearing site data.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of the request. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, WhatsApp and live project sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How we use your enquiry

We use the details you send only to reply to you and to deliver the work you ask for. We never sell or share them for marketing.

Client project data

When you hire us, we may handle data on your behalf, such as customer lists, form entries, ad audiences or account access. We use it only to deliver the agreed work, keep it secure with two-factor authentication and limited team access, and delete or return it when the project ends. Your pages, ad accounts, domains and hosting stay in your name.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years so we can follow up and answer later questions, and project records for as long as the law requires for accounting. After that we delete them.

Your rights

Under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to stop using it, or have it deleted. Email us and we'll respond within 21 working days.

Questions

Email eroxiilk@gmail.com and we'll answer, or delete anything you've sent us. See also our Terms.
